# Changes

## 0.4.0 — 2026-11-23

- Added death rules for ordinary bosses previously marked unsupported, including Spirit of Vengeance, False Emperor, Battle of Ilum and the Mandalorian boarding parties.
- Added scoped non-death endings for Foundry Revan, Meridian Malgus/Tau, Nul Malgus, Coratanni/Ruugar, Terror From Beyond and two Dxun encounters.
- Added the missing Infernal Council NPC IDs and 8/16-player death requirements. Debuff removal alone does not count as a win.
- Added main-boss rosters and faction alternatives. Bonus bosses do not block full runs. Activities with untracked mandatory objectives still cannot claim full coverage.
- Added Maelstrom Prison's X-37 Oppressor Droid and separated Lorrick's human and final forms.
- Fixed Kaon's third final-boss ID, Braxx's two hounds, Taral V's two vine cats and Cademimu's Veteran-mode ending.
- Preserved individual NPC damage contribution across a confirmed heal. An unexplained health refill still resets the contribution.
- Prevented malformed log gaps from joining boss deaths across attempts. Scripted victories require bounded, encounter-specific evidence and reject resets or missing health.
- Added batch log review with a short model brief, pseudonymized evidence and optional comparison with the application's counters. Original logs are read only.
- Updated catalog diagnostics and regression tests, including saved-history reprocessing.

After updating, close SWTOR and use **Settings → Reprocess saved history** to apply the new rules to older recordings. Keep the original logs available. Reprocessing does not change the logs.
